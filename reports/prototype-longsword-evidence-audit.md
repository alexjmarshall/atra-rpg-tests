## Method and grade proposal

`EARLIER` means a permitted earlier fifteenth-century witness; `RECONSTRUCTION` means the Atra candidate is a transparent synthesis from eligible period principles. Grades follow the packet's A/B/C/D rubric, but every grade below is a proposal. A grade evaluates the mapping between the named Atra candidate and its evidence—not merely whether a passage exists.

## Witness audits

### Zornhau-Ort

- **Record:** `play-german-longsword-zornhau-ort`
- **Names:** Atra game-facing name: **Zornhau-Ort**. Historical name(s): Zornhau, Zornhau, Ort. The Atra compound preserves the historical verse's named cut and immediate point, but presents them as one game-facing label.
- **Proposed inclusion:** **EARLIER / A**, PROPOSED pending review.
- **Witnesses:**

  - [Starhemberg Fechtbuch, Rome, Biblioteca dell'Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei e Corsiniana, Cod.44.A.8 (MS Cors.1449), anonymous Pseudo-Peter von Danzig gloss](https://wiktenauer.com/wiki/Pseudo-Peter_von_Danzig); 1452; **Longsword, ff. 13r.3-13v.1**. Wiktenauer digital edition: transcription by Dierk Hagedorn; draft English translation by Michael Chidester.
    - Source instruction, paraphrased: Against a right-side descending cut to the head, answer with a descending cut from the right onto the opponent's sword; if the opponent is soft at contact, extend the point to the face or chest.

- **Physical requirements:**

  - Weapon: Two-handed longsword.
  - Hands: Both hands remain on the sword; no free hand is described.
  - Contact/bind: Begins without contact; the counter-cut creates a bind, and the point follows if the opponent is soft.
  - Measure: Cutting measure closing immediately to point/thrust measure.
  - Guard/posture: Pre-fencing; the counter-cut is explicitly made from the right, but no named starting guard is given in this play.

- **Test Skill recommendation:** **Longsword — PROPOSED, not canonical.** The decisive historical action is performed with the longsword and requires ordinary two-handed blade control.
- **Current chassis comparison:** packet timing/type **Counter-attack**; audit cluster(s) **single-time-counters**; finding **match**. The source supports a counter-cut that simultaneously establishes an immediate point threat. The chassis must not assume any unapproved action-economy effect.
- **Review notes:** The manuscript metadata and digital edition credits are independently checkable on the Starhemberg Fechtbuch page: https://www.wiktenauer.com/wiki/Starhemberg_Fechtbuch_%28Cod.44.A.8%29

## Cross-candidate findings

- **Strong direct mappings:** Zornhau-Ort, Absetzen, Durchwechseln, Winden, Scambiar di Punta, and Pommel Strike have exact passages that closely match their current candidate identity.
- **Chassis wording needs review:** Zwerchhau is currently typed by historical category rather than procedural chassis; False Point currently describes the wrong initial threat; Rompere di Punta under-describes its close-play transition; Nachreisen compresses multiple pursuit moments; Crossing Throw consolidates two variants.
- **Anti-number caution:** Colpo di Villano's witness supports reception and return, not an anti-number doctrine. Cuts Against Many has direct Vadi principles but remains a reconstructed candidate rather than a named technique.
- **Skill/equipment result:** Longsword is the least-assumptive proposed Test Skill for all 13. False Point explicitly changes to a half-sword grip.
