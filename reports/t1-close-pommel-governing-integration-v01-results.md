## Governing Changes

The authoritative engine now exposes one E1 decision after a qualifying successful Tutta Cross and before H3 creation; implements T1's bounded state transformation; implements generic Close-opportunity Pommel P2; preserves pass/disengage and cap 3; and counts real point-threat transitions. Selector metadata and durable data match runtime.

## E1 T1 Timing

E1 occurs after the successful qualifying Basic Cross, including ordinary D1 timing, and before the original striker's H3 Rejoinder is created. Fühlen and D/M are unavailable during E1.

## T1 Decline / H3 Preservation

Declining T1 closes E1 and creates the same ordinary H3 Rejoinder that would otherwise have opened. A failed T1 gate opens H3 directly. If T1 is declared, H3 is never created; no cancellation fiction or event is used.

## T1 Cost / Chain

T1 costs exactly 1 Spiritus at declaration and one learned-Play chain entry. It makes no additional test, spends no additional normal action, and cannot exceed the global cap of 3.

## Wide -> Close

T1 retains the existing Crossing and changes measure from Wide to Close. It is not a generic close action and cannot be invoked from a hand-authored Wide state without the E1 gate.

## bind_height Cleanup

T1 sets `bind_height` to `Unknown`. This prevents Upper or Lower Winding only through their existing height gates; no special Winden prohibition was added.

## Hart / Weich Close Priority

Hart assigns the original striker the first Close opportunity. Weich assigns the Tutta defender the first Close opportunity. The T1 owner is therefore not guaranteed priority. Initial pressure clears immediately after assignment.

## Close Opportunity / Pass Semantics

The existing Bind Initiative field remains a first-declaration opportunity only. It is not ownership, Leverage, a modifier, or a new Close Initiative token. One pass transfers opportunity; two consecutive passes end Crossing; a declared continuation resets the pass count; existing disengage remains legal.

## Pommel Strike v0.2

Pommel Strike is a learned consumer of any already-valid Close Crossing. It requires the Play, both fighters alive, current opportunity, at least 2 Spiritus, and chain room. It does not require T1 or Tutta and does not create Close.

## Pommel P2 Cost

The governing selection is P2: 2 Spiritus and one learned-chain entry at declaration, with no additional normal action. P1 is rejected for governing use; the old 0-Spiritus/action-ready implementation is superseded.

## Pommel Response Model

Pommel adds no intrinsic response restriction. The runtime exposes the ordinary Cross/Beat/Counter/Ignore menu whenever the target actually has an action ready. In the normal E1 sequence both ordinary actions have already been spent, so practical immediacy follows from existing action economy, not a Pommel-specific denial rule.

## Pommel Hit

A hit uses a flat normal Longsword test, deals normal provisional longsword damage (`d6+1`), and ends the bounded bind sequence through normal cleanup. It does not set Open, grant control, disarm, grapple, or add a follow-up.

## Pommel Miss

A miss deals zero damage, retains Crossing at Close, sets height to Unknown, transfers the current opportunity to the opponent, clears the initiative-passed marker, and resets consecutive passes. It does not automatically separate.

## Pommel Generic-Close Trigger

Deterministic tests invoke Pommel from a separate authored-special Close Crossing as well as from T1. Both paths use the same legality and resolution methods.

## Old Pommel Supersession

The state-model fixture, continuation fixture, Play record, mechanical mapping, governing selector, and authoritative engine now supersede the old 0-Spiritus/action-ready/direct-denial description. Historical experiment reports remain unchanged for reproducibility.

## C0 Supersession

C0's raw/undefined invocation is noncurrent. A Wide Crossing object alone cannot declare T1; the authoritative E1 window is required.

## L1 Historical Control

L1 remains an archived experimental control. It is not exposed by the governing engine and was not rewritten into a governing record.

## Action Economy

The initiating Basic Cut spends the striker's action and Basic Cross spends the defender's action. T1 and Pommel spend no additional action. Tests preserve action flags through both declarations and report zero smoke/full-duel action leaks.

## Spiritus

T1 spends 1 and Pommel spends 2, both at declaration with legality checked first. Reserve boundaries behave as intended: starting at 1 permits T1 but no Pommel; starting at 2 leaves only 1 after T1; starting at 3 permits one T1-following Pommel. No underflow occurred.

## Learned Chain

T1 adds one entry and each Pommel declaration adds one. T1 plus two Pommel declarations fills cap 3; a fourth learned continuation is rejected. Basics and pass remain outside the learned chain.

## Information Visibility

Hart/Weich remains owner-private before E1. The original striker sees Unknown and cannot buy Fühlen before the T1 decision. After T1 uses pressure to assign Close opportunity, pressure clears and no Fühlen purchase is created.

## Beat Regression

Beat remains the universal displacement/separation defence and still sets the attacker Open only on success. In smoke scenario S8, 491 successful Beats produced **0 Close entries**.

## H3 Regression

## Winden Regression

## Scambiar / Rompere Protection
