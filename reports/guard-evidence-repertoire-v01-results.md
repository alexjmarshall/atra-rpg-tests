## Italian Guard Audit

### Posta di Donna

- Witnesses/locators: Fiore, Getty MS Ludwig XV 13 23v-b and 23v-d; Morgan MS M.383 12r-b and 13r-b; Pisani Dossi 18a-b. Earlier inclusion, early fifteenth century. Vadi MS Vitt.Em.1324 16r-d supplies direct-horizon continuity (c.1482-87).
- Direct: right Donna is high, can make all seven blows, cover all blows, break other guards through great blows, and exchange thrusts with specified offline footwork. Left Donna is ready to cover/strike, makes great blows, breaks thrusts down, and enters stretto.
- Geometry: the right-shoulder chamber and point-off-line reading come from the witness image, not a sentence prescribing an Atra state.
- Atra: ordinary cuts map to Basic Cut. Exchange of thrusts remains learned Scambiar di Punta. The named-guard harness now permits the existing Scambiar form from source-described Donna/Tutta access.
- Result: **STRONG CONCEPT SUPPORT** for a powerful/loaded-blow identity. Loaded mechanics remain provisional and Donna-only in this prototype; Vom Tag is not included by inference.
- Open: exact mechanics for left Donna's thrust-break/close sequence; the scoped Scambiar gate is not a claim that no other Fiore guard can ever exchange thrusts.

### Posta Frontale

- Witnesses/locators: Fiore Getty 24v-c, Morgan 12v-b, Pisani Dossi 18b-b; Vadi MS Vitt.Em.1324 Corona 17r-b and Frontale 17v-a.
- Direct: Fiore says Frontale is good for crossing; against a high thrust it crosses while moving offline; against a low thrust it beats the point down while moving offline. The same paragraph gives an alternate retreat, fendente, Dente di Zenghiaro, thrust, and return-fendente sequence.
- Naming boundary: Fiore explicitly says some masters call Frontale Corona. Vadi separately names and depicts Corona and Frontale. Therefore the names are related inside Fiore's testimony but are not silently interchangeable across traditions.
- Geometry: high/front-centred/cross-ready and not-threatening point are image-based state readings.
- Atra: high-thrust response = Basic Cross mapping; low-thrust response = Basic Beat mapping. The longer sequence is a learned candidate. Fiore's Frontale paragraph does not itself establish a generic crossing-to-close bonus.
- Open: review whether the packet's combined `Frontale / Corona Crossing` candidate should be split or renamed; no packet edit was made.

### Tutta Porta di Ferro

- Witnesses/locators: Fiore Getty 23v-a, Morgan 12r-a, Pisani Dossi 18a-a. Vadi's separately named flat-ground Iron Gate at 16v-a is continuity/context, not an automatic exact equivalence.
- Direct: a strong waiting guard; broad cover claims; exchange of thrusts; beat thrusts to the ground; cover with a pass and enter stretto.
- Geometry: low/grounded and point-off-line combine text and witness-image evidence.
- Atra: simple cover maps to Basic Cross; simple downward thrust displacement maps to Basic Beat. Scambiar remains learned and is guard-gated in the scoped harness. Cover plus stretto is a learned continuation candidate. No Strong Cover bonus was created.
- Open: cover-to-stretto mechanics and whether future access should be a nonexclusive affinity rather than the current bounded hard gate.

### Mezza Porta di Ferro

- Witnesses/locators: Fiore Getty 22r-a and 24r-b; Morgan 12r-d. Vadi MS Vitt.Em.1324 16r-c supplies direct-horizon continuity.
- Direct: Fiore includes Mezza among guards that stand ready to thrust; it throws strong thrusts, forcefully beats swords upward, returns with fendente to head/arms, returns to guard, and is difficult to break/close against without danger.
- Textual ambiguity: Getty and Morgan differ on wording concerning desired sword extension/length. That variation is preserved and does not affect the directly supported thrust capability.
- Atra: point-ready maps to the existing threatening-point state and is implemented. Simple thrust = Basic Thrust. Minimal upward displacement = Basic Beat. Beat plus return cut = learned candidate. No safety/defence bonus was created.

## German Guard / Breaker Audit

### Alber

- Witness/locator: Starhemberg Fechtbuch, Cod.44.A.8 (MS Cors.1449), anonymous Pseudo-Peter von Danzig gloss, 1452, f. 26r.2.
- Direct posture: right foot forward, arms extended, point on the ground, short edge upward.
- Geometry/Atra: low and not-threatening are transparent state mappings from that posture. The source does not supply a passive invitation bonus.
- Invitation/deception: no item-level invitation teaching was found in the scoped witness. If later evidence establishes one, it should first be assessed as a learned Play trigger rather than a passive benefit.

### Krumphau -> Ochs

- Locator: Cod.44.A.8 ff. 16v.4-17r.1; confirming four-guard list f. 26r.5.
- Meaning: against left Ochs, set left foot forward, hold at the right shoulder, spring right/offline, and strike over the hands with crossed arms and the long edge.
- Current record: exact-locator-verified. Current mechanics: unimplemented. A gate/bonus alone is not adequate; the entry needs a narrow Play specification.

### Schielhau -> Pflug

- Locator: Cod.44.A.8 ff. 23r.3-23v.1; confirming list f. 26v.2.
- Meaning: the named inverted-sword hew breaks Pflug and breaks forcefully into cuts/thrusts.
- Current record: exact-locator-verified. The active S2 Schielhau is a reactive descending-cut/change-through-denial form. It does not yet model the proactive Pflug breaker. Later redesign is required; no modifier was added.

### Scheitelhau -> Alber

- Locator: Cod.44.A.8 ff. 24v.3-25r.2; confirming list f. 26v.3.
- Meaning: spring in against Alber with a strong descending long-edge cut to the head. If Crown receives it, the passage continues through point-sinking, winding, pressing, and slicing.
- Current record: exact-locator-verified. Current mechanics: unimplemented. The breaker and Crown continuation need a narrow Play specification.

### Verify Zwerchhau -> Vom Tag

- Locator verified: Cod.44.A.8 ff. 18v.2-19r.1; confirming list f. 26v.1.
- Meaning: preempt the high Vom Tag/descending attack with an offline spring, hilt covering before the head, and short-edge strike to the head.
- Current record: promoted to exact-locator-verified. Current mechanics remain unimplemented. No automatic breaker modifier was added.

In this gloss, the four `Versetzen` are described as four hews that break the four guards. “Break” is therefore a sourced cut/entry relationship, not automatic success, Boon, Bane, damage, or defence cancellation.

## Basic Mapping vs Learned Play

The accepted G1 rule yields a clean split. Ordinary single-effect Cross, Beat, Cut, and Thrust descriptions remain universal Basics. Joined counterthrusts, cover-to-close transitions, beat-return attacks, retreat-cut-thrust chains, breaker entries, Winden, Absetzen, and Nachreisen remain learned.

The existing Scambiar mechanics cleanly match Donna/Tutta exchange-of-thrust descriptions, so the named-guard harness now gates that implemented form to those source-described guards. This is a bounded prototype access rule, not an assertion that the historical technique was exclusive to them.

## Distinct Basic Guard Actions

**NONE are justified.** Every surviving simple behavior is expressible through existing Basic Cut, Thrust, Cross, or Beat. Every behavior not so expressible contains timing, compound payload, measure change, or a learned technical lesson.
