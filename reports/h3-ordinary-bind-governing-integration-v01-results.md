## Governing Changes Made

The authoritative shared engine now separates ordinary H3 state from preserved special/local contested relations. New ordinary state includes owner-private `initial_pressure`, public scalar `bind_height`, a narrow Rejoinder window, current bind opportunity, and finite pass sequencing. Existing operators were sufficient; no generic mechanical operator was added.

Durable synchronization covers the governing register/YAML, H3 data record, Crossing/Bind state model and schema, effect vocabulary, D/M, Winden, Zornhau, Ochs/Pflug, the vertical-slice grammar map, current governing tests, and generated repository audit metadata.

## Ordinary Basic Cross

Before rolling, the parrier secretly declares Hart or Weich and supplies the authored defence-event geometry. Hart gives exactly one existing defence Boon; Weich is flat. Failure leaves no Crossing, initial pressure, height, or Rejoinder. Success cancels the incoming attack, establishes ordinary Crossing, stores private pressure, writes public authored height, and opens the scoped striker Rejoinder. It adds no point threat, guard change, Leverage, generic modifier, or bonus damage.

## Favored/Unfavored Supersession

Ordinary Basic Cross no longer compares the successful attack/Cross rolls to write Favored/Unfavored. There is no H3/R0 fallback. The relation type remains because qualifying Zornhau continues to write and consume it locally.

## Initial Hart/Weich

`hart | weich | unknown` is a separate, phase-scoped information axis. The parrier can inspect their own declaration; the striker receives `unknown` unless paid Fühlen reveals it. D/M declaration or Rejoinder decline consumes/clears pressure. Winden never rewrites it.

## Bind Height

`upper | lower | unknown` is public, scalar, independent of measure, contact zone, pressure, point threat, and guard, and supplies no modifier.

## Upper Writer

Only explicit `upper-cross-against-descending-cut` defence metadata against a qualifying descending/Oberhau-like Cut writes Upper. Starting in Ochs, merely targeting high, or using an arbitrary Cross does not.

## Lower Writer

Only explicit `lower-setting-aside` defence metadata against a qualifying low-line thrust or rising low-line cut writes Lower. Starting in Pflug alone is insufficient. The integration reuses defence-event metadata and adds no “Low Parry” Basic or new control operator.

## Unknown Geometry

All nonqualifying ordinary Cross geometry deterministically writes Unknown. Unknown retains the Rejoinder, Fühlen, D/M, opportunity, other legal authored repertoire, pass, and Disengage. It has no Upper/Lower Winding, random height, choose-a-height, or Favored/Unfavored fallback.

## Bind Rejoinder

The original striker receives one insertion after the scoped successful ordinary Cross. Its options are paid Fühlen when legal, Duplieren, Mutieren, or decline. Arbitrary learned Plays are rejected. It neither refreshes nor spends another normal action. Authored attacks that prohibit attacker continuations retain that protected restriction rather than acquiring this insertion.

## Fühlen Ordinary-Bind Semantics

Once per live initial cycle, a fighter who knows Fühlen may spend 1 Spiritus to reveal the opponent's current Hart/Weich. It costs no action and no chain entry and changes no pressure, initiative, geometry, roll, attack, or defence.

## Duplieren / Mutieren

D/M is one learned repertoire item with two Rejoinder branches. Each declaration requires Wide Crossing, 2 Spiritus, and chain room; it consumes one learned entry and no additional action. Correct Duplieren/Hart makes a Booned high Cut for normal damage. Correct Mutieren/Weich makes a Booned low-opening Thrust for normal damage while retaining its winding transition and threatening point. A wrong read spends 2S and the entry, rolls no attack, deals zero damage, and performs normal cleanup—no chip, Open, Damage Boon, or response restriction was added.

## Bind Initiative and Pass Semantics

On decline, Hart assigns the striker the first ordinary opportunity and Weich assigns the parrier, then pressure clears. Bind Initiative is sequencing only: no exclusive ownership, bonus, or Leverage. A first pass transfers one opportunity; a second consecutive pass clears Crossing. Passing costs no action, Spiritus, or chain entry. An actual continuation resets the pass count.

## Winden Repertoire

Winden is a learned family rather than a universal maneuver. Current ordinary executions are Upper Winding Thrust and Lower Winding Thrust. Every declaration consumes one learned-chain entry. Zornhau-local W1/W2 remains separate.

## Upper Winding Thrust

Requirements are known Winden, current opportunity, Crossing, Upper, 2S, and chain room. Declaration spends 2S/+1 chain/no action, retains Crossing, establishes Ochs/upper hanging and threatening point, and makes a flat normal-damage Longsword Thrust. Hit ends the bounded sequence under normal cleanup. Miss deals zero, retains Crossing/Upper/Ochs/threat, and transfers opportunity.

## Lower Winding Thrust / L2

Requirements and accounting match Upper but require Lower. Declaration establishes Pflug/lower hanging and threatening point and makes a flat normal-damage upward Thrust. Hit ends the bounded sequence. The governing L2 miss deals zero, retains Crossing/threat, changes Lower to Upper and Pflug to Ochs, and transfers opportunity; the original actor receives no free follow-up.

## Ochs / Pflug Transitions

These guards are authored Winding aftermath, not prerequisites or height writers. Upper establishes Ochs. Lower establishes Pflug; only an L2 miss moves that sourced aftermath to Ochs while changing height to Upper.

## Action Economy

The initial attack and Cross use their normal actions. Fühlen, D/M, pass, and Winding add no action and never refresh an action. Tests assert both fighters remain without a hidden second normal action through three-continuation miss chains.

## Spiritus

Fühlen is fixed at 1S; each D/M or ordinary Winding declaration is fixed at 2S. Costs are paid at declaration. Deterministic reserves `0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8` confirm the 2S legality boundary. No integration tuning changed these values.

## Learned Chain

D/M and each actual Winding add one entry; Fühlen and pass add none. The fixed cap is three. A Lower miss → Upper miss → Upper declaration can fill all three entries; the fourth learned continuation is illegal.

## Point Threat / D1

Ordinary Cross itself adds no threat. Mutieren and the two Winding executions author threatening point as specified. Existing state-based D1 remains otherwise unchanged: a threatening opposing point denies it; ordinary Crossing/form name alone does not. Zornhau's authored threat and D1 denial remain local and intact.

## Zornhau Local Preservation

Qualifying Zornhau still cancels, establishes its authored Crossing/threat/initiative, compares its two successful rolls for the local Favored/Unfavored relation, and supports current intrinsic Ort and local W1/W2 behavior. Ordinary H3 state does not enter this path.
