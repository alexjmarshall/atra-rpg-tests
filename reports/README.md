# Reports

Generated validation artifacts live here. Rebuild them with:

```powershell
python scripts/validate_repository.py --write-reports
```

Reports identify missing research and implementation data. They are not authorization to infer or fill unsupported mechanics.

The four primary audits are:

- `source-audit.md`
- `duplicate-chassis-audit.md`
- `curriculum-coverage-audit.md`
- `skill-equipment-audit.md`

Chassis and curriculum coverage classifications are reproducible research heuristics. They do not modify Play records or establish mechanics.
