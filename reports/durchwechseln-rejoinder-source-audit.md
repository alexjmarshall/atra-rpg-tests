# German Longsword Rejoinder to Durchwechseln

Status: **historical evidence accepted; mechanics PROVISIONAL**

## Selected candidate: Schielhau long-point denial

- **Atra record:** `play-german-longsword-schielhau`
- **Historical names:** `Schilär`, `Schilhaw`; Atra uses normalized `Schielhau`.
- **Witness:** anonymous Pseudo-Peter von Danzig gloss in the Starhemberg Fechtbuch, Rome, Biblioteca dell'Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei e Corsiniana, Cod.44.A.8 (MS Cors.1449), 1452.
- **Exact location:** longsword Schielhau text and gloss, **ff. 23v.1-23v.2**.
- **Edition/translator:** Wiktenauer digital edition; transcription by Dierk Hagedorn; draft English translation by Michael Chidester.
- **Checkable text:** [Pseudo-Peter von Danzig digital edition](https://wiktenauer.com/wiki/Pseudo-Peter_von_Danzig).
- **Instruction, paraphrased:** Against a descending cut to the head, turn the sword and strike long with extended arms and the short edge above the opposing sword. If the opponent aborts or misses against that cut and tries to change through below, continue shooting the point long forward so the change-through cannot pass beneath it.
- **Inclusion proposal promoted:** `EARLIER / A` for historical identity.

## Why this candidate

The passage names Schielhau and gives a concrete physical answer to change-through. It is stronger evidence than inventing a generic Counter-Feint chassis. The prototype therefore treats the point-extension as a continuation/rejoinder belonging only to the Schielhau Remedy against the witnessed descending-cut context. It is not available after arbitrary defences or against unrelated deceptions.

## Corroborating constraints

The same gloss's Durchwechseln section, ff. 30v.3-31v.2, says change-through should be used against blade-seeking defences before a bind, warns that the opponent may set-on during the change, and advises against changing through when the opposing point remains before the face or openings. This supports the long-point denial logic but does not create a separate generic counter technique.

No final action relationship, damage rule, Spiritus cost, tier, or card wording is claimed by this source audit.
