## Committed / Counter

Committed is public on any authored attack. The target may Counter or use authored Preparation before the roll; removal cancels, survival lets the attack proceed. Waiting preserves ordinary post-success simultaneous Counter. A miss creates no retroactive Counter; only authored Recovery applies. Power is one user.
