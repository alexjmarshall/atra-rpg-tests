The v0.4 packet reports reproducible simplified-model results, but it also keeps engagement/access rules, residual defence, anti-number Play mechanics, guard architecture, and the exchange-chain timing contradiction OPEN. Future simulations should version their assumptions and must not treat an OPEN or PROVISIONAL choice as adopted.

Before adding a simulation, record:

- governing packet version;
- adopted, provisional, and experimental assumptions separately;
- exact rules/model scope;
- seed or exact-solver method;
- acceptance question;
- generated output location.
